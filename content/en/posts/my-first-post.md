Welcome to my brand-new blog! This is a personal space built with **Hugo** and the **PaperMod** theme.

## Why Hugo?

- **Blazing fast builds**: Hugo is the world's fastest static site generator, with millisecond build times.
- **Easy to maintain**: Every post is written in Markdown, which makes version control effortless.
- **Plenty of themes**: There is a huge selection of open-source themes to switch between.
